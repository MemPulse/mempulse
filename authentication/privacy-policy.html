Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you use MemPulse, we may collect information you provide, such as your name, email address, password, notes, and flashcards. We may also collect basic technical information, such as your browser, device, and how you use the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage your account, provide and personalize study features, save your learning content, communicate with you, improve MemPulse, and protect the service from misuse.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate MemPulse, or when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer.

4. Your content

Your notes, flashcards, and other study materials belong to you. We process and store them only to provide the service and its features. Please avoid adding sensitive information that is not needed for your studies.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

MemPulse may use cookies or similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, understand service usage, and maintain security. You can manage cookies through your browser, though some features may not work correctly if they are disabled.

6. Data retention and security

We keep information for as long as needed to provide MemPulse, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

7. Your choices

You may review or update account information, request deletion of your account, or stop using MemPulse at any time. Some information may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate operational purposes.

8. Children’s privacy

MemPulse is not intended for children who are not permitted to use online services under the laws that apply to them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page and change the date above. Your continued use of MemPulse after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy can be sent to the MemPulse support team through the contact details provided in the application.

Questions, answered

Can I use MemPulse for free?

Yes. The Free plan includes deck and flashcard creation, spaced repetition, daily review sessions, and basic progress tracking. You do not need a paid plan to build a study routine.

What is the difference between monthly and annual Plus?

The proposed Plus features are the same on both plans. Monthly is ₱79 per month, while annual is ₱599 for a year—about ₱50 per month and around 37% less than twelve monthly payments.

Are all Plus features available today?

No. This page is a pricing concept. Advanced features marked as planned need to be developed and tested before they are advertised as available in a live paid plan.

Can I pay on this page now?

Not yet. The paid-plan buttons demonstrate the selection flow only. A payment provider, secure server-side payment verification, and subscription management must be connected before checkout can go live.

Questions, answered

Can I use MemPulse for free?

Yes. The Free plan includes deck and flashcard creation, spaced repetition, daily review sessions, and basic progress tracking. You do not need a paid plan to build a study routine.

What is the difference between Plus and Pro?

Plus adds deeper insights and study planning for busy semesters. Pro is built for exam season and heavy course loads: it adds PDF capture, higher-limit AI card tools, exam-date adaptive scheduling, sharing, and full backups. Everything in Plus is included in Pro.

How much do I save with annual billing?

Plus is ₱79 per month or ₱599 for a year (about ₱50 per month, around 37% less than twelve monthly payments). Pro is ₱149 per month or ₱1,149 for a year (about ₱96 per month, around 36% less).

Are all paid features available today?

No. This page is a pricing concept. Features marked as planned need to be developed and tested before they are advertised as available in a live paid plan.

Can I pay on this page now?

Not yet. The paid-plan buttons demonstrate the selection flow only. A payment provider, secure server-side payment verification, and subscription management must be connected before checkout can go live.
